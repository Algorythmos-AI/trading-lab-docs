## Signal words

The desk can give each focus name one word: CALL, PUT, WAIT or NO TRADE. The words are built and switched off.
They come on only after DEC-0051 Amendment 1, which needs at least 10 dry sessions recorded after all four of its
preconditions are met. Two are met. A third is merged and waits to be confirmed in production. The fourth, one
full session of board-feed stamps, is not met yet.

## On the page

- Brief, Live and Review views; keyboard navigation; a command palette; a single-pane phone layout.
- A page per name with linked zones and compact rows.
- A count of how far the delayed feed and the reference feed agree.
- Focus cards, the Trade now board, a trade ticket, a manage card for open trades, and a link to the
  [day review](/stocks-options/day-review).
- The card record. It is scored after each close by `trading-focus-card-ledger`. A session with no saved card is
  listed as missed, with its reason, and never filled in silently.

## The fill-time check

A one-off check that the times in your trade journal are the times your orders really filled. It matches each
journal time for an option trade to the public option tape for the same contract, and compares that with windows
a few minutes either side, where a real fill time should match far less often.

- **Runs once, started by you.** There is no button and no schedule. From the signed-in site, send one
  same-origin `POST` to `/api/desk/fill-check` with the body `{"confirm":"run-once"}`. A `GET` on the same path
  shows the result. Once a result is stored, asking again returns it and runs nothing.
- **Keeps counts and seconds only.** The stored result holds session dates, counts, rates, time offsets in
  seconds, reasons a trade was not checked, and a one-word verdict. No price, contract or result in money is
  stored or logged.
- **Until it runs**, the `GET` answers `not_run`. Whether it has been run is not recorded in the repository.
