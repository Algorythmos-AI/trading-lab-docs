The Day review (`/review`) puts one New York day of your own option positions against your rules, on one screen.
It reads the same journal as the Trades page and takes every figure from the Trades page's own functions, so the
two pages agree about the same day. The one exception is at the end of this page.

It opens on the latest day with a closed call or put. Add `?day=` and a date (written 2026-11-02) to open
another.

## The four tiles, in order

| Tile | Value | Line under it |
|---|---|---|
| **Rules kept** | Positions with no rule broken, of the day's positions | The day's trade cap and loss limit, kept or broken |
| **Loss size** | Dollars lost beyond −25% of premium, added up over the day's losers | How many losers went past the stop, how many lost over $50, and how many are left out |
| **Result** | The day's result after fees | Its R50, the result before fees when the fees are an estimate, and a note when it counts sales that are not listed |
| **Trades** | Positions taken | Won, lost and still open |

The order is on purpose. A day can make money and still break the rules or let a loss run, so the page asks
how the day was traded before it says what it paid.

## The two loss sizes

- **Beyond −25% of premium.** A loser sold for less than three quarters of what it cost, by at least a cent. The
  tile adds up the dollars past that line. The test is on the price you sold at, before fees.
- **A loss over $50 before fees.** Rule 4's limit on one trade. A big position can lose more than $50 without
  passing −25%, so the two are counted apart.

Only when nothing is left out and neither happened does the tile give the all-clear: "Every loss at plan size."
when there were losers, otherwise "No losers today." or "No losers this day."

## R25 and R50

- **R25** is a result in quarters of the premium paid: −1 is exactly the −25% stop.
- **R50** is a result in units of $50, the most one trade may risk.

Because the −25% test is before fees, a result a little under −1 R25 can still be inside the stop.

## Positions

The day's positions in the order you opened them: **Opened**, **Closed**, **Contract**, **Contracts**, **Cost**,
**Result**, **R25**, **R50** and **Rules**. On a phone each is a card of three lines. A loser past the stop
carries a second line with the dollars "beyond the plan". **Rules** reads **Rules kept**, the rules broken
("Rule 5", "Rules 5, 7"), or **Open** for a position still held with no rule broken yet.

## The rules legend

Under the list, each rule broken that day appears once, in number order, with its short title as the Trades page
writes it, for example "Rule 5: Stop at the chart, −25% at most". Numbers alone do not read.

## Previous and next

The two links in the header go to the nearest days either side that have positions. A side with none has no link.

## Words on the page

| Words | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Still open** | Contracts of this position are still held. It has no result, and it is never shown as rules kept. |
| **left out** | A closed loser the Loss size figures cannot count: it has no cost, or the broker's history has a gap there. A day with one is never given the all-clear. |
| **as last read** | Today, when the journal is not current. The Rules kept line gives the figures as of the journal's last read time, not as kept. |
| **Session still open** | Today, during the session: this is the day so far. |

## What you will not find

- **No after-the-sale prices.** The page stops at your exit, so the day is judged on what you did.
- **No win percentage.** A day of small rule-keeping losses is not made to look worse than it is.
- **No split by board state or time.** Every figure covers the whole day, so no slice can flatter it
  (DEC-0045, DEC-0049).

The page also says board labels are not being recorded yet. The labeller is built but switched off, so board
labels are not recorded yet.

## The one difference from the Trades page

A contract sold in full at a loss, bought again the same day and still held at the end of it:

- The **Day review** counts the first position as a closed loser and lists the rebuy as its own open row.
- The **Loss size** panel on the Trades page treats the whole contract that day as still open and leaves the
  first position out of its figures.

On such a day the two loss counts differ. Otherwise the page's tests hold its figures equal to the Trades page's
on every day of the test journal.
