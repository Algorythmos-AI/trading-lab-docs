## In one paragraph

The oldest platform and the only one running every trading day. It holds a research engine, a stocks desk that
scans, signals, and trades one strategy on an Alpaca **paper** account, an options desk that prepares levels,
rules and option arithmetic for your own decisions, and the status site that shows every desk.

The crypto desk has left it (closed 2026-10-24); renaming the repository `options-lab` is the owner's call.

## Reading this honestly

No strategy here has shown an edge. The one stocks candidate failed its backtest gate, and its re-run on corrected
data is accepted (DEC-0011) but has no experiment record yet. Forward evidence is being collected because that is the next written
step, not because a result is expected.
