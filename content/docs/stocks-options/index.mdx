## In one paragraph

The oldest platform and the only one running every trading day. It holds a research engine, a stocks desk that
scans, signals, and trades one strategy on an Alpaca **paper** account, an options desk that prepares levels,
rules and option arithmetic for the owner's own decisions, and the status site that shows every desk.

The repository will be renamed `options-lab` once the crypto desk has left it.

## Reading this honestly

No strategy here has shown an edge. The one stocks candidate failed its backtest gate and is being re-evaluated
under a decision that is still proposed. Forward evidence is being collected because that is the next written
step, not because a result is expected.
