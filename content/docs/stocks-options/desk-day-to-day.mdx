You trade long calls and puts by hand in your broker's app. The Options page sits beside it, on the laptop or the
phone. It reads prices and your journal, works out your own rules, and tells you where each name stands. It places
nothing. Session times on it are New York time; the one exception is the line under the desk that says when the
levels were published, which gives Sydney time.

## Before the open

**The focus cards.** Under **Today's focus** there is one card per name picked after the close, in rank order. On a
phone the cards sit side by side and you swipe; on a laptop they stack, two across on a wide screen. Each card reads
top to bottom:

| On the card | What it is |
|---|---|
| The rank and the name (#1 SPY) | Where the name sits on the card's list. |
| **Breakout watch**, **Hold support** or **Range: wait** | The card's lean for the day. |
| The state chip | The board's live word for this name (see below). |
| The pre-market line | Where the name sits before the open, its pre-market price and the gap from the close. |
| The ladder | The levels drawn to scale, the grey no-trade box between the two triggers, and the live price. |
| **CALL above** and **PUT below** | One strip per side: the trigger price, the live distance to it, then **In ≈** (the limit price to aim for), **Stop** (the chart stop, or −25% on the option) and **T1** (the first target). |
| Under the strips | **How each side works**; a row of other triggers, tagged on the page as under test, with **What the other triggers mean** folded under it; a fold for a plan that is under test; **Draw on chart**; and **Size this call** or **Size this put** (the trade ticket). |

**The pre-open check.** The card is refreshed with pre-market prices at 08:50 and should be in by 09:05. The line
beside **Today's focus** then reads **Pre-market check** and the time. Before that it reads **Picked** and the time
of the evening build.

**The rules strip** under the cards shows the session's fixed times and limits: no entries before 09:35, none from
11:30 to 13:00, out after 5 minutes if the trade is not working, at most 5 trades a day. Be flat by 15:10, or 12:10
on a half day.

**Feed and age.** Three places say where prices come from and how old they are:

- The feed line on the board: the name of whichever feed the board is reading (the code's default is IEX),
  that prices are fetched every 2 seconds rather than streamed, how long ago the last trades printed, and when the
  page last got an answer. A name with no trade today is counted apart.
- The feed health strip under the rail: one chip each for the levels, the pre-open refresh, the live prices, the
  session bars, your journal and the server check, each with its state (such as **On time**, **Waiting**,
  **Late** or **Failed**) and its age.
- The hero's price line, which says what the price is: **live** and its time, **last trade**, **pre** or **close**.

**"Paused"** means the prices are too old to act on. In the session, if the page has had no answer for 20 seconds,
or a name's last trade is more than 2 minutes old, that name reads **PRICES PAUSED**. The hero greys out and says
no entry is shown on old prices. Check your broker's app instead.

Look at: the cards' triggers, the pre-market line, and the strip's chips.

## From 09:35: the Trade now board

Before 09:35 the board is folded away, and the hero says when entries start and shows the #1 name's two triggers.
From 09:35 every focus name has a state, but the board itself draws a row only for a name worth acting on: a
**GET READY**, an **ENTER ZONE**, a live other trigger, or a name whose **I'm in** form you have open. The most
urgent comes first. Every other name is counted in one line that sends you to the glance above, and names whose
prices are paused are listed apart. Each name's word is always on its chip in the glance and on its card.

| State | Where you see it | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| **NO TRADE** | Glance and card chip | Nothing to do now, for one of these reasons: too early, the midday pause, too late to enter, your day's limit, or price sitting mid-way through yesterday's range. |
| **WATCH** | Glance and card chip | Price is away from both triggers. The card shows the live distance to each. |
| **GET READY** | Board row, glance, card | A trigger has broken, or price is at it. Wait for the 2-minute close and a retest that holds. |
| **ENTER ZONE** | Board row, glance, card | The break held its retest. The row shows the entry, the stop and the first target. |
| **PLAN OFF** | Glance and card chip | Price went back through the level. That side's plan is off. |
| **MISSED** | Glance and card chip | The retest window passed, or price already ran past T1. Don't chase. |
| Other triggers | Board row, glance, card | Tagged on the page as under test, with their own numbers. |
| **PRICES PAUSED** | A line on the board, glance, card | Prices too old to judge. |

The **Now** panel at the top shows the most urgent name large, with its three numbers as tiles. **Cockpit** turns
the page into one full-screen board for a second monitor or a tablet: the rail, the hero and the four ladders,
with the notices still on screen. Its switch is on the rail, on the phone too.

What you do in your broker's app is never automated. The site places no order, holds no order and cannot see an
order you place.

Look at: the hero's word and side, its cautions, and the rail's clock to the next boundary.

## Before buying: the trade ticket

Open **Size this call** (or put) on the card, or the hero's size button. Type the option's price per share as your
broker quotes it: 1.20, not 120. The site's own option chain is 15 minutes behind, so its numbers never feed the
ticket.

With no price typed, a table shows **The most a share you may pay** for 1 to 5 contracts, under the $200 premium
cap and with the $50 stop rule. Once you type a price it shows **Contracts**, the stop to place (−25%, rounded up
to the tick), the loss if filled at the stop with fees, the fees and the total premium. For example, a typed 1.20
gives one contract and a stop of 0.90. A price that breaks a rule is said not to fit your rules, with the reason.

Estimates at the chart stop and T1 appear only when you also pick a strike and an expiry, the page has a live stock
price, and a volatility can be solved from your price; there are none under $0.05 or within a tick of the option's
intrinsic value.

Look at: **Contracts** and the stop to place. Check the tick in your broker.

## In the trade: the manage card

Tap **I'm in** on an entry row, or wait for your broker's fills to reach the journal (the card says these are up to
2 minutes behind). A card titled **In a trade · SPY call** then shows one word, large:

- **HOLD**, **TRAIL**, **SELL HALF** or **EXIT**, with one line on what to do;
- **CHECK** when the card cannot judge the trade: prices paused or off, or a note the journal's fills do not show.

Under the word sit:

- the stop;
- how far the stock is from the stop;
- the **Not working in** countdown (rule 6, 5 minutes);
- the trail;
- the time to be flat by;
- a **P&L** label that points you to your broker. The manage card shows no live P&L for your own trades.

Add your option fill (price a share and contracts) and the card shows the stop to place in your broker. Tick the box
that says the stop is placed once you have placed it. The tick is yours: the page cannot see your broker. Under it
the card says the room left today if this stop fills, such as "If this stop fills: $50.00 of your $100 day limit
left.", or why it cannot say.

An **I'm in** note and its tick live in that browser only. A trade noted on the laptop does not show on the phone
until the journal has the fill.

## Stopping: Done for today

**Done for today** sits in the rail. One tap asks "Stop for today? Entry prompts will be hidden on both devices.",
and **Confirm** saves it on the site. **Undo** shows for 60 seconds.

Once set, the rail reads **Done for today · set** and the time, on the laptop and the phone. Every entry prompt is
hidden: names read **DONE TODAY**, a name you are still in reads **IN A TRADE**, and the hero shows no tiles and no
size button. Manage cards, stops and exit words stay exactly as they were. The other device picks it up within 30
seconds, or when you return to its tab. If a read fails, the rail says **Could not check**, never that the day is
open.

## After the close

Outside market hours, when the Options page shows the fold with the last card's review, it also shows the line
**Day review: your last day against your rules**. See [the day review](/stocks-options/day-review).

The **Trades** page holds your own closed trades in four groups:

| Group | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The rules and the room left, then this session's trades as they close, and a link to the day review. |
| **The record** | Every closed trade for the period, kind and ticker you pick: totals, **Loss size**, patterns and **Every trade**. |
| **History** | The record over time as a calendar, and **Money** in and out of the account. |
| **The clone** | The paper bot that trades your rules with its own paper money. |

## What the site will not do

- **No orders.** Its broker access is read only. It cannot place, hold, change or cancel an order.
- **No advice.** The ticket and the card are your own rules worked out, not a recommendation.
- **No prompts on your phone.** No entry or exit prompt for your own trades reaches the phone. **Sound** and
  **Browser notification** work only while the page is open in that browser.
- **Nothing after your exit.** No page shows what a trade did after you sold it.
- **No board labels yet.** Which board state you traded from is not recorded; that labeller is not built.
