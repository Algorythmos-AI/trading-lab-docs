<Callout title="Paper trading only" type="warn">
  Every platform trades paper money. One job reads your own Webull account, read only: its client refuses any order
  request before it is sent. No repository places a real-money order.
</Callout>

Three platforms, each in its own repository, one shared knowledge base, and a few supporting repositories
([the map](/start-here)). Only Stocks & Options runs on the cloud server and reports to the
[status site](https://lab.algorythmos.com). Crypto is closed and Forex HFT is parked.

## The platforms

| Platform | Trades | Built | Standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Stocks & Options](/stocks-options) | US stocks and long options | Scanner, signals, backtester, risk, order manager, Robot B (paper), nightly jobs, options workstation | <Standing is="open">forward evidence in progress</Standing> |
| [Crypto](/crypto) | Crypto, 15-minute to 4-hour bars | Desk, own simulator, three sleeves, challengers, weekly learning loop | <Standing is="failed">closed 2026-10-24: no rule passed its first gate</Standing> |
| [Forex HFT](/forex-hft) | Currency pairs, seconds to minutes | Code for every planned phase; the cash float funded on the paper account | <Standing is="waiting">parked since 2026-10-14</Standing> |
| [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base) | Nothing: sources and verified claims | Two collections built and tagged | <Standing is="open">`options` not started; `crypto` not needed since the desk closed</Standing> |
