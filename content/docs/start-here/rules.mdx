title: The rules
description: What holds on every platform, whatever the strategy.
---

## Paper only

- Broker adapters are locked to paper accounts in code, and tests hold the lock in place.
- The settings that would allow real money belong to the owner alone.
- Each platform has a kill switch. While it is on, no new position is opened; open positions are still managed.

## Decide before you look

- A strategy or evaluation change is written down first: a hypothesis, an experiment, a decision record.
- The record is committed before any affected result is viewed.
- Experiments are append-only. A re-run gets a new id. A holdout is used once per frozen configuration.

## One platform, one repository

- One platform per pull request. No imports across platforms.
- Research ids carry the platform: `HFT-` for forex, `CRY-` for crypto. A reference to another repository is
  written `repo:ID`.

## What may be written down where

| Class | Examples | In these pages |
|---|---|---|
| Restricted | Specification text, setup names, decision and hypothesis prose, experiment reports, knowledge-base text | Never |
| Private | Balances, host notes, log lines | Yes, behind the login |
| Safe | Counts, ids, statuses, dates, statistics | Yes |

These pages show research records as ids, statuses and dates only.

## Changes

Every change goes through a branch and a pull request, and merges only after its checks pass.
