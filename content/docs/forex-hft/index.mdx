## In one paragraph

A fully automatic platform for the currency market. It records every price update, decides, passes every order
through an independent risk gate, keeps two books, retrains at the weekend, and replaces a model only when it
passes tests written down beforehand. The broker is Interactive Brokers, **paper account only**.

"High frequency" here means many trades a day held for seconds to minutes. It does not mean microsecond races
or co-location.

## What it waits on

1. **The broker login.** The paper login is shared with another routine; two sessions on one login end each other.
   That clash must be resolved first.
2. **A new decision record** that restarts the desk, with either a host install or one clean day on the laptop.
3. **Then the gates as before.** The reconciliation break of 13 October is still open, the control sleeve needs one
   clean day in shadow, and several gates need five recorded trading days.
