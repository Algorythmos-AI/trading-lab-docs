## In one paragraph

A fully automatic platform for the currency market. It records every price update, decides, passes every order
through an independent risk gate, keeps two books, retrains at the weekend, and replaces a model only when it
passes tests written down beforehand. The broker is Interactive Brokers, **paper account only**.

"High frequency" here means many trades a day held for seconds to minutes. It does not mean microsecond races
or co-location.

## What it waits on

1. **A choice by the owner** on how to hold positions. The paper account refuses spot purchases without the
   paying currency in hand, refuses short sales, and cannot hold yen. The options are cash balances in each
   currency, or CFDs. Until that is decided the trader stays in shadow.
2. **The host.** A pre-flight day of measurements, the install, the broker gateway and its weekly login.
3. **Time.** Several gates need five recorded trading days, and the learning gate needs two unattended weeks.

See [phases and gates](/forex-hft/phases) for each one.
