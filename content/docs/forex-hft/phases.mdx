## What code cannot close

F2, F3, F4 and F6 need days or weeks of real operation. Writing more code does not move them.

## Decisions still to be written

There are **no decision records yet**. These values are placeholders until one sets them:

- risk limits
- promotion rules for sleeves and for models
- every number in the cost model
- drift thresholds
- the weekend training and promotion times

## The order of work from here

1. Owner chooses cash balances or CFDs, and the F1 decisions are recorded.
2. Pre-flight day on the host, then the install.
3. Recording starts. Data only accumulates with time, so this starts as early as possible.
4. After five recorded days: the F2 and F3 gates.
5. Risk limits decided; control sleeve at minimum size for five days: the F4 gate.
6. Strategies and the weekly learning loop on real recordings.
