## The week

| Loop | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Record | Sunday 17:15 to Friday 17:00, New York time | Every price update for the traded pairs goes to an append-only store |
| Decide | Continuously | Features, then model scores, then sleeves, each with its own book and limits |
| Guard | Before every order | The risk gate rebuilds positions from fills and owns the kill switch |
| Trade | Continuously | Paper orders on the broker's paper account |
| Account | Continuously | Two books are kept side by side |
| Learn | Weekend, market closed | Rebuild datasets, train candidates, validate, register |
| Promote | Weekly | A challenger replaces the champion only if it passes tests set in advance; automatic rollback if it then breaches its loss limit |
| Report | Daily and on every change | Alerts, a daily summary, a weekly scorecard |

## Two books

- **The broker book** is what the paper account reports.
- **The shadow book** applies the platform's own latency, spread and slippage model.

The shadow book is the result of record. A paper fill is easier than a real one, so the conservative book is
the one that counts.

## Guardrails

- The paper lock refuses any connection it cannot prove is a paper connection.
- One piece of code may place or cancel orders. A test scans the source to keep it that way.
- An order is written to the outbox before it is sent, so a crash cannot lose track of it.
- The recorder is a separate process from the trader and cannot place an order.
- A sleeve starts in shadow and moves up one stage at a time, by a rule written beforehand, or not at all.
- At its limits the trader first stops entering, then flattens, and never leaves a position unwatched.

## On the host

It will share the existing cloud server with the other platforms, under its own user, its own unit prefix and
its own resource slice.
