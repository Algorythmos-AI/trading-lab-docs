## Run 1: 2026-10-11, read-only

Minutes after the weekly open. Recorded as `HFT-EXP-0007`.

- Four pairs each resolve as spot and as a CFD: the euro, the pound and the Australian dollar against the
  US dollar, and the US dollar against the yen.
- Five levels of market depth are available, and historical ticks.
- No order was placed.

## Run 2: 2026-10-12, with four one-unit test orders

Asian session. Recorded as `HFT-EXP-0008`.

| Measured | Result |
|---|---|
| Top-of-book updates | About 1 to 3 a second |
| Median spread | 0.2 to 0.4 pip, one thirty-second sample per pair |
| Test orders sent | 4 |
| Test orders accepted | 0 |

**Why the orders were refused.** On this paper account, spot currency cannot be bought without holding the
currency that pays for it, cannot be sold short, and yen cannot be held at all.

## Run 3: 2026-10-12, the float funded

Recorded as `HFT-EXP-0009`. From the owner's laptop, not the host. Every sleeve stayed in shadow.

| Measured | Result |
|---|---|
| Orders sent | 7 market orders, one at a time, each through the risk gate and the outbox |
| Accepted and filled in full | 7 |
| Commission | Charged in the account's own currency, at the broker's published minimum on every order; it arrives as its own report a few milliseconds after the fill |
| Cash reconciliation | Exact, commissions included |
| Position reconciliation | Held until the gateway's first reconnection, then a break |

These are the platform's first accepted orders and first fills. It tests no strategy and counts as no trial.

## What this changed

- The design assumed quotes every few milliseconds. The measured rate is a few a second.
- The trader now refuses any stage above shadow while the account cannot open the positions a sleeve needs.
- The choice was made: spot on a cash float. Limits, the cost model and the control sleeve's first step are
  now set by decision records, and a runbook covers running it from a laptop. It has not yet run for real.

## Not yet measured

The commission above its minimum, slippage, CFDs, order timing from the host, and whether cash and
positions agree after a reconnection. The probe runs' spreads do not feed the cost model.
