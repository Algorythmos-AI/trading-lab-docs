Asian session. Recorded as `HFT-EXP-0008`.

| Measured | Result |
|---|---|
| Top-of-book updates | About 1 to 3 a second |
| Median spread | 0.2 to 0.4 pip, one thirty-second sample per pair |
| Test orders sent | 4 |
| Test orders accepted | 0 |

**Why the orders were refused.** On this paper account, spot currency cannot be bought without holding the
currency that pays for it, cannot be sold short, and yen cannot be held at all.

## What this changed

- The design assumed quotes every few milliseconds. The measured rate is a few a second.
- The trader now refuses any stage above shadow while the account cannot open the positions a sleeve needs.
- F1 now waits on a choice between holding cash in each currency and trading CFDs.
