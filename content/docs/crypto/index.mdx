## In one paragraph

A desk that traded crypto on paper, every 15 minutes, all week. It reads public market data only, fills orders
in its own simulator, runs a tournament of rule variants in shadow, and retrains a model weekly. It has **no
exchange credentials and no way to send an order**: it is paper by construction.
