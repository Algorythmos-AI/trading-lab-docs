## Around the cycle

| Job | When | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Bar cycle | After every 15-minute close | The chain above |
| Snapshot | Every 15 minutes | Publishes the desk's status |
| Challengers | Daily | Retires, draws at most two a week, registers, backtests, admits |
| Learning | Daily, with weekly retraining | Scores signal outcomes, runs the model's promotion tests |

## Paper by construction

There is no exchange account, no credential and no order route. The simulator is the only place a trade
happens, and its costs are stated assumptions in the frozen configuration.

## Design choices

- **A frozen configuration, pinned by hash.** A test fails if it changes without a new record.
- **Limits per desk and per sleeve,** with a latch that stays on until it is reset.
- **Challengers run in shadow** and enter only through a registered backtest gate.
- **The snapshot format belongs to this repository.** The status site keeps a copy checked by hash.
