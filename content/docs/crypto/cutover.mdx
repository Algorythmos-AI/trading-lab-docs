## How the cutover goes

1. Read-only pre-checks.
2. Preparation.
3. The cutover itself, in a slot of about 12 minutes between two bar cycles.
4. Verification.
5. Rollback, if verification fails.

Some steps in the runbook were written without host access and are marked as unverified.

## Why the removal waits

the old repository.
